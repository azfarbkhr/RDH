# Research and investigation reports

Use for substantial research, investigation, issue, or summary reports. When findings need a durable presentation, also load `build-useful-html-artifacts`.

For a substantive report, use this default order:

1. **Conclusion:** The answer or current best assessment.
2. **Scope:** The question, system, records, and period examined when needed.
3. **Findings:** Evidence grouped by impact or decision value, not discovery order.
4. **Assessment:** Root cause or interpretation, labeled when uncertain.
5. **Next steps:** Impact, decisions, owners, risks, and open questions that require action.

Use only sections with real content. Collapse a short report into paragraphs rather than preserving empty headings. Keep observations separate from interpretation, place evidence under the finding it supports, and remove repeated discussion and dead ends.

When rewriting user-provided text, preserve its meaning, factual claims, nuance, and established terms. Flag any substantive interpretation needed to complete the rewrite.
