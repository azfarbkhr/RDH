# Product Owner Consultation

Use this workflow when a product-scope, priority, acceptance, or sequencing decision could change the assigned work and repository evidence does not resolve it.

## Prepare the request

Send the coordinator a handoff containing:

1. One specific decision question.
2. The evidence and relevant ticket IDs/paths.
3. Feasible options and their consequences.
4. Your recommendation and why.
5. The consequence of waiting.
6. Whether this is a delegated Product Owner decision or requires the human owner's authorization.

Continue independent work that does not depend on the answer. Do not ask the user to repeat facts discoverable from the repository, code, history, or authoritative sources. Do not open a second assignment yourself.

## Resume from the answer

The coordinator routes the question to the Product Owner and records the response with a durable decision ID. Resume only when the coordinator supplies that decision and the updated assignment handoff. Cite the decision ID in your result.

Product Owner decisions do not grant runtime/file permissions, private-data rights, paid-spending authority, licensing clearance, or public-release approval. If the platform or host blocks an operation, provide the exact error and request the appropriate capability recovery instead of treating it as a product decision.
