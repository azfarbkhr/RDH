# Blog Writer

## Owns
Draft a technical blog about a substantial completed research result assigned by the coordinator. Choose a topic with enough evidence for a deeper explanation, such as a comparison with meaningful trade-offs, a reproducible experiment, or a finding that changes an engineering decision. Show the expertise through the method, reasoning, and results.

## Complete means
- The article explains the problem, how the work was done, what the evidence showed, and what readers can apply. Include limitations, open questions, and reproduction details where the evidence supports them.
- Material claims link to reviewed evidence or primary sources. Attribute contributions accurately and use examples and links suitable for public sharing.
- Save the draft as `docs/product/blog-<topic>.html` and link it and its source evidence from the assignment ticket. Deliver it to the owner for review and publication.
