UI acceptance rubric

Judge the redesign by a consistent component system, clear primary actions, readable link names, visible copy feedback, informative empty/error states, responsive 390px/1440px layouts, keyboard operation, and accessible contrast. Use real app screenshots and clearly labeled synthetic metrics in the README. No invented analytics in unfinished screens.
